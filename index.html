1Get your Quest ready (one time)

  1. Turn on Developer Mode: create a (free) developer organization at developer.oculus.com, then in the Meta Horizon phone app go to Devices → your headset → Headset settings → Developer Mode → On and reboot the headset.
  2. Plug the headset into this computer with a USB cable.
  3. Put the headset on and accept “Allow USB debugging” when prompted (check “Always allow”).

2Install from this page

Works in Chrome or Edge on desktop. Click, choose your Quest in the pop-up, and the game installs straight over USB — no other software needed.

Trouble connecting? Close SideQuest and anything else that uses ADB (or run adb kill-server), unplug/replug the cable, and make sure you accepted the USB-debugging prompt inside the headset. If your browser doesn't support WebUSB, use the manual install below.

3Manual install (fallback)

⬇ Download TridentTide.apk
  1. Install SideQuest (Advanced Installer) on your PC.
  2. Connect the Quest by USB and accept the debugging prompt in the headset.
  3. Drag the downloaded TridentTide.apk into SideQuest, or use “Install APK file from folder”. (Command-line folks: adb install TridentTide.apk.)

After installing, find the game in the headset under Library → Unknown Sources → Trident Tide.

How to play

SwimHold grip and pull yourself through the water — alternate hands to build speed. That's the only way to move; thumbsticks just snap-turn.
TridentReach over your right shoulder + grip to draw it from your back.
StabThrust it into an enemy to stun them. After recovering, everyone is briefly shielded — no stun-locking.
ThrowFling and release — it stuns on impact and loyalty-returns to your back like a certain enchanted trident.
StealSwim into the enemy cavern and grab the pearl out of their giant clam.
PassPearls can be thrown — release grip mid-swing.
DefendGrab your own loose pearl and it flies home instantly.
ScoreCarry the enemy pearl into your own clam (your pearl must be home). First team to 2 round wins takes the match.
Steal windowGrabbing the enemy pearl grants a short shield so you can break out of their base.
AnnouncerA voice calls every play arena-wide — thefts, returns, captures and the final result — with a small subtitle instead of screen-filling text.
MenusFour terminals ring the lobby spawn — point and pull trigger: Match (1v1/2v2/3v3, three difficulties), Appearance (body, trim, trident), Victory Fanfare (pick which fanfare plays on a capture, with preview) and How to Play.
